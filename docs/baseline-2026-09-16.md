## 6. Ranked bottlenecks (numbers justify rank)

### Decode @4K (27.56 ms/tok clean)
1. **Trunk matvec — 22.43 ms (66.7%), 11.133 GB @496 GB/s (78% of 640 GB/s peak).**
   Bytes × bandwidth dominates everything; closing to LM-head rate (627 GB/s) saves ~4.7 ms (−17%).
2. **Activation quant (q8_1 row quant) — 1.89 ms (5.6%).** Pure overhead per token, next single item after head.
3. **LM head — 1.39 ms (4.1%), 0.874 GB @627 GB/s (98% peak, i.e. NOT optimizable via BW).**
   Attention is only 4th (1.27 ms, 3.8%, cache-resident @1.27 TB/s emitted).

### Decode @64K q4_0 (48.06 ms/tok instrumented)
1. **Attention — 18.80 ms (39.1%), 1.206 GB unique @64 GB/s (issue-bound dequant).**
   Now the #1 bottleneck, not matvec; q4_0 KV is slower than f16 here (+2.6 ms) — R4 territory.
2. **Trunk proj matvecs (ffn_gate_up 9.04 + ffn_down 5.25 + gdn_proj 4.23 + out_proj 1.95 ≈ 20.4 ms, 42.5%)**
   — same bytes as @4K (11.133 GB) but relatively diluted; rate ≈ 545 GB/s, still below head rate.
3. **LM head — 1.40 ms (2.9%) @~625 GB/s: at ceiling, not a target.** GDN delta+norms ≈ 1.9 ms (4%).

### Prefill 512 (355.81 tok/s best; 4096: 310.77 tok/s)
1. **Staging-bound tiled GEMM** — prefill throughput only −12.7% from 512→4096 tokens
   but absolute ~311 tok/s ≈ 8× decode; per brief §8.2.2 staging uses ~23% of fetched line ≈147 GB/s
   + activations vs 400–500 GB/s target). Full attribution needs stage-counters (PENDING).
2. **GDN 2-pass DRAM re-reads** — decode share small (delta 0.86 + norms ~2.8% ≈ 1.9 ms/tok),
   but prefill replays the recurrence over all 512/4096 rows; fusion (R5) targets +10–20% of GDN prefill slice.
3. **Attention over growing prefix** — O(T²)-ish; decode @pos 4090 shows only 1.27 ms
   (cache-resident), so in prefill-512 its share grows with T; chunk-128 caps activation/KV working set
   (~0.87 MB/tok × 128 ≈ 109 MiB). Exact split PENDING level-2 prefill instrumentation.
